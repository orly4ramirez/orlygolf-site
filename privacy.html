OrlyGolf

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026. Operator: Orly Ramirez.

Who we are

OrlyGolf is a golf scorecard app run by Orly Ramirez. This page explains what information the app uses, why we use it, and how you can ask us to delete it.

Your account

You can create an account with an email address and password, or by using Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. When you use Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from that service so we can set up your account.

The golf information we store

To run the app and keep your information available if you get a new phone, we store your golf data with a cloud service called Supabase. That includes:

  • Your profile, such as the name on your scorecard and your handicap
  • Your rounds and scores
  • Course cards you have saved
  • Groups and competitions you join, and the leaderboards for those groups

People in a group or competition with you can see your name and scores there, the same way they would on a shared paper card.

If you leave sharing turned on in your profile, a course card you scan or type yourself (the printed course details, not your scores) may be offered to other OrlyGolf players so they can use the same course. You can turn this off anytime in your profile.

Photos

A profile photo you add stays on your phone. We do not upload it.

When you photograph a paper scorecard so the app can read it, the picture is sent through our server to Google Gemini, which reads the card and sends back the details. We do not keep a copy of the photo on our servers, and the photo is not included when your rounds are saved to the cloud. The picture itself stays on your phone.

Caddy O

When you ask Caddy O a question, we send your question, recent chat turns, and some context about your round — such as the course, how you are scoring, your handicap, your club distances, and a few stats — through our server to Google Gemini so it can answer. Your chat history stays on your phone. We also keep a simple count of how many questions you have asked so the caddy can work fairly.

Finding a course

When you search for a golf course, our server looks it up with GolfCourseAPI (GolfCourseAPI.com). If that does not find the course, the app may also look it up using OpenStreetMap.

What we do not do

OrlyGolf does not show ads. We do not sell your information. The app does not include tracking or analytics tools from other companies.

Deleting your information

In the app, you can delete a round or remove a saved course card from your library.

To delete your OrlyGolf account and the information we store with it, email us at orly4ramirez@yahoo.com. We will remove your account and the data tied to it.

Children

OrlyGolf is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request to delete your account, can be sent to Orly Ramirez at orly4ramirez@yahoo.com.
